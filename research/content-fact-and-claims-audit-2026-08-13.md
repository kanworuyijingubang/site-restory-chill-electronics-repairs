# Content fact, citation and claims audit

Audit time: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai)  
Target: `lib/site-data.ts`, 12 approved content routes plus trust/legal routes.

## Checks

- Release date, developer, publisher, platforms, minimum requirements and achievement count trace to the official Steam store/appdetails or official Steam achievement endpoint.
- All 50 achievement names and descriptions are presented as an official checklist. The strings “First reviews!”, “Getting popular!” and “Best shop in Akiba!” refer to in-game achievement requirements, not current Steam review totals.
- Gameplay sequences, device competition reference and ending routes use visible `Official`, `Community`, or mixed evidence labels and link dated source pages.
- Live regional price, live discount and current review totals are not hardcoded.
- No page claims an official console/mobile port, official mod support, complete ending count, promo-code system, best-profit device, universal winning build or exact hidden odds.
- Two achievement-linked ending routes are described without converting them into a total ending-count claim.
- The 29-device list is labeled as a competition reference, not as a complete in-game inventory.
- Unsupported piracy/crack/trainer/download intents remain outside public content.
- Patch-sensitive observations carry a 2026-08-13 update boundary and a community-mechanics freshness note.

## Result

- Unsupported critical claims: **0**.
- Stale critical claims: **0**.
- Visible uncertainty/source boundary defects: **0**.
- False-positive string review: three “reviews” matches are official in-game achievement descriptions and are safe to retain.
