# Brand asset and commercial-use record

All launch brand assets are original code-authored geometric artwork created for ReStory Repair Desk in this project:

- `public/favicon.svg`: original compact circuit/tool mark.
- `public/icon.svg`: original maskable app icon.
- `app/opengraph-image.tsx`: original generated social-card composition.
- CSS workbench illustration: original HTML/CSS geometry; no third-party game art or screenshots are embedded.

The launch version did not redistribute Steam artwork, community screenshots, video frames or competitor assets. Source links were textual. The project owner may use and modify those original assets for the site, including commercial advertising-supported use.
