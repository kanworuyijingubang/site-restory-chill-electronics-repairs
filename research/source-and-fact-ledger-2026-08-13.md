## Official achievement facts used in content

The official Global Achievements page provides names and unlock descriptions for all 50 achievements. Examples used for task routing include:

- `Clean Job`: clean the workshop.
- `Big cleaning!` / `Galactic cleaning!`: remove dirt and dust from 100 / 1,000 parts.
- `Flipper` / `Garage sale!`: buy 5 / 25 devices at the marketplace.
- `Making money!`: sell 5 devices.
- `Hacking 101` / `11001`: reprogram 1 / 25 devices.
- `Custom orders available!`: paint a device for a client order.
- `Promise of Akiba`, `Star of Akiba`, `Legend of Akiba`: win one, three and at least one competition for every device.
- `Rock for the ages!` and `Globalization`: hidden descriptions on the official page; route details require clearly attributed community evidence.

Global percentages are volatile and will not be hard-coded in article copy.
