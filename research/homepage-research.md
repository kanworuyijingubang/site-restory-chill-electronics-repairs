## Four first-entry questions

1. How do I complete my first repair and grow the shop?
2. Why is a part still dirty, missing or impossible to place?
3. How do firmware, paint and stickers unlock?
4. What should I check when cleaning, progress, saves or controls stop responding?

The homepage links these questions to approved canonical routes. Methodology terms such as evidence gate, source boundary, watch item and verified route are kept out of player-visible copy.
