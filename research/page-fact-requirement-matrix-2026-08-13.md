## Global unknown and conflict list

- Console/Switch/PlayStation/Xbox ports: no official confirmation in the checked launch sources.
- Official mod support: no official confirmation in the checked launch sources.
- Exact number of endings: current search results conflict; only two official achievement-linked routes are documented.
- Exact economy values, best-profit device, competition scoring formula and universal winning build: patch-sensitive or unsupported.
- Current price, discount and review totals: volatile; use the live Steam page.
- Promo/redeem codes: no evidence-backed code system found; no codes page is in the launch scope.
- Demo-to-full-release save transfer: not confirmed in the checked official sources.

Unknowns are publication boundaries. They must not be converted into claims or filled from model memory.
