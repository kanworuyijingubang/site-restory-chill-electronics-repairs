# Competitor selection and page-family gap report

Observed 2026-08-13 from live result pages and public sitemaps.

## Selected result competitors

1. `chillelectronicsrepairswiki.wiki` — direct new-game guide competitor; public robots and 256-URL sitemap captured.
2. `restory.wiki` — direct entity/device wiki competitor; public robots, sitemap index and 196-URL child sitemap captured.
3. Steam Community Guides — platform-native competitor with 10 current guides; no sitewide sitemap was crawled because the domain is not dedicated to this game.
4. Destructoid endings guide — editorial competitor visible for the endings query; no dedicated game sitemap was found or inferred.
5. TheGamer achievement guide — editorial competitor visible for the achievement/endings result set; no dedicated game sitemap was found or inferred.

## Public sitemap findings

- `chillelectronicsrepairswiki.wiki`: 256 URLs; one apex plus 63 English and 64 each in Japanese, Russian and Spanish. The sitemap uses a single lastmod date of 2026-08-07 for the observed pages. Families include guides, devices, tools, parts, customers, story and achievements.
- `restory.wiki`: sitemap index lastmod 2026-07-30; 196 child URLs, mostly `/w/` MediaWiki routes. Many are files and categories; a much smaller subset is player-facing entity/device content.
- Steam, Destructoid and TheGamer were selected for result-quality/content-depth comparison, not for extracting their global sitemaps.

## Page-family gaps to exploit

- A compact task router that answers cleaning, selling, reprogramming/customization, achievements, competitions and endings without sending users through asset/category pages.
- Explicit evidence labels separating official Steam facts from current player-tested tips.
- A spoiler boundary for ending pages.
- A live-price boundary that avoids stale regional prices.
- A focused English launch instead of instant thin translation.
- No fabricated device specifications or assembly order. Device detail pages remain gated until each page has reproducible evidence.

No competitor article text or images are copied.
